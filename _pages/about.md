To address any issue which fundamentally challenges a person's life or their way of living, it is best for those who are threatened to be informed about the risks they face, and they should feel as though they can do something about it. The development of artificial intelligence is one such fundamental problem, whose capacity for dramatic change will reshape societies and industries, and will even lay siege to our very understanding of what it means to be human. Those who work in AI safety seek to address the many concerns that emerge from this technology. Currently, although full of brilliant people, the field is young, disconnected, and difficult to approach as an outsider.

Frontier Watch aims to address this issue. On this site, you can expect to read regularly published, thoughtful works by interesting people who write about AI safety in a highly accessible, motivating way. Selecting any article, the goal is that you can read it, understand it and feel informed, get a sense of current solutions, and feel as though you yourself can contribute to solving the problem if it is something that interests you. Although primarily a journal, this site offers plenty of resources that you can make use of to meaningfully get involved in AI safety, and to make a difference in solving some of the most fascinating, and challenging problems our species has ever been faced with. Humanity needs you, good luck!
